About Our Club

The Tororo university students One Voice 27 is a faith-based.
platform created to bring people together through the message of God.
It provides a space where people can learn,share, and be encouraged in their faith.

Our members come from different destinations and we share the word of God together

Through preaching the gospel, sharing communial work like helping the needy .

Our Mission

To use digital technology to share God's message, encourage spiritual growth, and connect people through faith.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Objectives

Our purpose is to spread the word of God, encourage people in their spiritual journey, and promote unity among believers. Through One Voice 27, we believe that people can come together with one voice to share God's message of love, hope, and faith.

Help students develop practical technology and digital skills in the all world.
