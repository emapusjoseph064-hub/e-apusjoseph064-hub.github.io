Club Activities

CHARITY

Charity Charity is an activity where One Voice 27 students show love and care by helping people in need. This may include visiting vulnerable people, sharing food, clothes, scholastic materials, or other useful items. Charity teaches students to serve others and demonstrate compassion in the community.

PRAYER AND WORSHIP

Prayer and Worship is another important activity where students come together to communicate with God through prayer, songs of worship and thanksgiving. It creates an opportunity for students to seek God's guidance, express gratitude and encourage one another spiritually.

BIBLE STUDY

Bible Study Bible study is a gathering where students read, discuss and learn from the Bible. Members share their understanding of Scripture and relate biblical teachings to their everyday lives. It helps students grow in faith, understand God's word and encourage one another.

PREACHING

Preaching Preaching involves sharing God's Word and Christian teachings with other students or members of the community. It encourages people to understand Christian values such as love, forgiveness, honesty, faith and service. .
